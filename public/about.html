Pursuit

Chase big goals, one small task at a time. Pursuit is a minimalist focus app. It always shows you the next small thing to do, so the big goal keeps moving.

Open Pursuit

What it does

  • Goals: set something you want to achieve by a date. Each goal gets a grid with a box for every day until the deadline.
  • Milestones: mark targets along the way on the grid.
  • Tasks: break the work into small steps. Home shows one task at a time, with a timer, quick logs, notes and a plan of action.
  • Logs and streaks: log what you got done; the day's box gets brighter, and a daily streak keeps you coming back.
  • Meetings: optionally connect Google Calendar to see this week's meetings and free time next to your tasks.

How Pursuit uses your Google data

  • Sign in with Google gives Pursuit your name, email address and profile picture, used only to identify your account and sync your goals and tasks across your devices.
  • Google Calendar (optional, read-only): if you choose to connect it, Pursuit reads your calendar events for the week you are viewing and shows their title, time and meeting link on the Meetings page. Events are fetched by your browser, are not stored on Pursuit's servers, are never changed, and are not shared with anyone.
