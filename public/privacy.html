Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Pursuit is a small focus app for goals, milestones and tasks. This page says what it stores and why.

What Pursuit stores

  • Your account: when you sign in with Google, Pursuit receives your name, email address and profile picture, and uses them only to identify your account.
  • Your content: the goals, milestones, tasks, notes, logs and timer sessions you create. They are saved in your browser and, when you are signed in, in Pursuit's database so they sync across your devices.

Google Calendar

If you choose to connect Google Calendar on the Meetings page, Pursuit asks for read-only access to your calendar events. Your browser fetches this week's events directly from Google and shows them to you. Calendar events are not saved to Pursuit's database, are never changed, and are not shared with anyone. The access token is kept in your browser for the current session only.

Pursuit's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What Pursuit does not do

  • No selling or sharing of your data with advertisers or other third parties.
  • No advertising and no tracking for advertising.
  • No use of your data to train AI models.

Where data is kept

The app is hosted on Vercel. Sign-in and synced data are handled by Supabase. Each account can read and write only its own data.

Deleting your data

In Pursuit, open Settings and choose "Reset my data" to erase your goals, tasks and notes. You can remove Pursuit's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have your account removed entirely, write to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
